I’m Jon Reyna, a software engineer in the Tampa Bay area of Florida. I build distributed systems that keep large fleets of infrastructure in the state they’re supposed to be in: a supercomputer’s network at Cray, 2,317 store Kubernetes clusters at The Home Depot, and connectivity between Equinix Fabric and the three big clouds at Equinix. For thirteen years that has meant Go, C++, C and Java, and most recently infrastructure for AI inference and AI agents. The [work page](/work) has the details.

My university training is in computer science, mathematics and statistics, and the mathematics never left. I’m now going deeper into how models are trained and served, from first principles.

<h2 id="this-site">This site</h2>

Writing is where I work through what I’m learning. The essays borrow their apparatus from Knuth’s *The Art of Computer Programming*: numbered equations, algorithms set step by step, and rated exercises with answers at the back, because working the exercises is how I learn. Play holds interactive pieces, for ideas that are easier to understand by moving them.

<h2 id="contact">Contact</h2>

The [résumé](/resume) has the particulars. Write to me at [jon@jonreyna.dev](mailto:jon@jonreyna.dev); I’m also on [GitHub](https://github.com/jonreyna) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jonreyna).

<h2 id="colophon">Colophon</h2>

The site is static: Markdown built into HTML with [Jaspr](https://jaspr.site), a Dart web framework, and served by Cloudflare. Reading pages carry no JavaScript, and math is MathML, set when the site is built. The design takes after an old English hardback printed in red and black. The type is Alegreya and Alegreya SC, with Courier Prime for code and Libertinus Math for formulas.
